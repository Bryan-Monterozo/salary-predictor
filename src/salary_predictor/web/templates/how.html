OUR APPROACH

Evidence first.
Always.

Salary numbers can tell different stories. We make the distinctions visible.

01

Start with your context

Enter your current job title, experience, education and relevant skills. You'll review the details before continuing.

02

Different evidence, different meanings

Advertised salaries are what employers list. Government wage averages summarize groups of workers. A personalized estimate requires a separately evaluated, approved model.

03

Only claim what is supported

No approved model currently serves the website. The local research prototype and historical wage tables are not automatically converted into personal PHP predictions.

04

Grow responsibly

Future releases may include new occupation mappings, reviewed skills and calibrated estimates. Today's shell is intentionally honest when salary information isn't available.
